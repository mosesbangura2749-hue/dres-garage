DRES GARAGE – Delete your data or account

DRES GARAGE is the vehicle service reminder app of Divine Rock Engineering Services Limited (Freetown, Sierra Leone), published on Google Play by Moses Bangura.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to divinerockengineering@gmail.com with the subject "DRES GARAGE data deletion request".
  2. Include: your name, the phone number the garage has for you, and your vehicle number plate. Staff members should include the email address they sign in with.
  3. Say whether you want all your data deleted, or only reminders stopped.
  4. We may contact you on that phone number to confirm the request is genuine.
  5. We complete the request within 30 days and confirm by email or WhatsApp.

You can also ask in person at the garage: 2nd Building, Earth Station, Wilberforce, Freetown.

What is deleted

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your vehicle details, odometer readings, service history and part records
  • Your booking requests and notes
  • Reminder messages logged for you and any push-notification token
  • For staff: your sign-in account (email and password) and staff profile

What may be kept

Invoice numbers and amounts for work already carried out may be kept in the garage's accounting records for up to 7 years where required for tax and legal purposes. They are not used for reminders or marketing.
