DRES GARAGE – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

DRES GARAGE ("the app") is a vehicle service reminder app operated by Divine Rock Engineering Services Limited, 2nd Building, Earth Station, Wilberforce, Freetown, Sierra Leone ("we", "us"). The app is published on Google Play by Moses Bangura on behalf of the company. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

1. Who uses the app

Garage staff sign in with an email address and password to manage customer vehicles, service records and reminders.

Customers of the garage open a personal link sent to them by the garage on WhatsApp. They do not create an account or a password.

2. Information we collect

  • Customer details: name and phone number (WhatsApp number), entered by garage staff.
  • Vehicle details: number plate, make/model, year, fuel type and usage type.
  • Service information: odometer (mileage) readings, service dates, parts replaced, service notes, invoice numbers and amounts charged.
  • Booking requests: preferred service date and an optional short note the customer writes.
  • Notification data: if a customer allows notifications, a technical push-notification token for their device.
  • Staff account data: staff name, email address and role.

The app does not collect location, contacts, photos, files, payment card details, or advertising identifiers. It contains no advertising and does not use third-party analytics or tracking.

3. How we use the information

  • To calculate when each vehicle and part is due for service.
  • To send service reminders and booking confirmations to customers by WhatsApp or in-app notification.
  • To let the garage see which vehicles are due or overdue and keep service history.
  • To let staff sign in securely.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Sharing

Information is shared only with service providers that run the app for us:

  • Supabase – secure database and sign-in hosting.
  • Netlify – hosting of the app itself.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) – delivery of reminder messages to the customer's own WhatsApp number.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The database uses access controls so that customers can only see their own vehicle and staff must be signed in.

6. Retention

We keep vehicle and service records for as long as the customer uses the garage, so that service history stays accurate. Records are deleted on request (see below) or when no longer needed for business or legal purposes.

7. Your choices and rights – including deleting your data

Customers can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete their information, or to stop WhatsApp reminders. Staff can ask for their account and data to be deleted. Email divinerockengineering@gmail.com with your name, phone number and vehicle plate (or staff email) and we will act on the request within 30 days.

8. Children

The app is intended for vehicle owners and garage staff. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this address, with the date at the top.
